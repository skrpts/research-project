## Research Project

This project starts with the pipelines research work repeats, in the order the work tends to arrive: survey the field, synthesise it, keep the references straight, prepare to be reviewed.

### What is installed, and when to reach for it

**Literature Review Pipeline** — at the start. Surveys the existing literature and maps what is already known, including where the field disagrees with itself. Reach for it before committing to a question.

**Systematic Review Pipeline** — when the method calls for it. Synthesises evidence against a stated protocol with screening and extraction. Optional, and deliberately so: a systematic review is a specific methodology, not a step every research project takes.

**Citation Management Flow** — throughout. Keeps references consistent, checks in-text citations against the reference list, and finds the gaps. Required, because citation errors are the ones that survive to publication.

**Peer Review Preparation Flow** — before submission. Prepares a manuscript and anticipates the objections a reviewer will raise. Optional: not every project submits.

### The order they tend to run in

```
literature-review  ->  systematic-review  ->  peer-review-prep
         |                                           ^
         +--->  citation-management  ----------------+
```

A tendency, not a dependency. Each pipeline runs on its own.

### What is deliberately not here

**No note-capture pipeline — yet.** Capturing structured notes while reading belongs in this shape and a stage for it is coming. Until one is ready to earn its place, this template leaves it out rather than including something that would not do the job.

### What this project does not assume

- **No reference manager is connected.** Citations are handled on content you supply.
- **No database access.** The literature pipelines work on sources you provide rather than searching publishers directly.
- **No particular referencing style.** Tell the citation pipeline which you need — Harvard, APA, Chicago, MLA or Vancouver.

### Making it yours

Give the citation pipeline your target style and the review pipelines your field's conventions. Both are ordinary content you author in the app.

If a pipeline here does not match your methodology, remove it — particularly the systematic review, which is the most method-specific of the five.
